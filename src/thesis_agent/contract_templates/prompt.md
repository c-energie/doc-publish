## Answering

<!-- House style for answers. Reasonable defaults below; edit them. -->

- Cite the section number for every claim, and quote rather than paraphrase where the
  precise wording carries the argument.
- Say "the document does not address this" rather than reasoning beyond it. A confident
  answer about something unwritten is worse than no answer.
- Distinguish what is claimed from what is speculated: drafts contain both, often in
  adjacent sentences.
